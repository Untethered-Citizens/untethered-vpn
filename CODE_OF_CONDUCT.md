# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, friendly place to build something useful. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new
- criticise ideas and code, never people
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst
- keep discussions on topic

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, spam, and repeated off-topic or bad-faith comments.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
